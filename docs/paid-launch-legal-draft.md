# Paid-beta legal/privacy decisions — review required

Unpublished preparation for The Wired Nomad LLC, trading as Acquisition Companion / Acquisition Companion Deal Lab. Initial paid customer jurisdiction: United States only. The public course remains free. No approval to activate accounts or subscriptions is implied.

## Confirmed commercial draft

One product, $19/month or $190/year in USD, recurring until canceled. Includes up to 100 AI requests per UTC calendar month on either interval; reset at 00:00 UTC on the first day, no unlimited claim or free trial. Model attempts count even when unsuccessful; invalid/access/quota-denied requests do not. Final Checkout disclosures and price/tax/mode matching must be verified before purchase.

Cancel recurring billing any time through Manage billing/Stripe Portal. Access continues through the already-paid billing period unless applicable law requires otherwise. Do not configure immediate cancellation by default. Current code denies past_due/unpaid/canceled/expired access and restores it from verified canonical billing state after recovery; agree support handling before launch.

Owner-selected refund draft: “Subscription fees are generally non-refundable after access has been used, except where required by applicable law. If you believe you were charged in error or have a billing problem, contact support@acquisitioncompanion.com.” This is not legal advice or a finalized policy. Counsel/owner must resolve unused access, annual plans, erroneous charges, outages and mandatory rights. No blanket 14-day promise or unconditional no-refunds rule.

## Account and data review

Store email/account identity, hashed browser-bound single-use 15-minute sign-in challenges, expiring hashed sessions, Stripe references/status and metadata-only AI usage. SES sends fixed plain-text auth email; Stripe hosts payment entry. No card storage or private deal/CIM uploads. OpenAI requests remain store:false, without a zero-retention guarantee. No chat content is saved in D1. Account pages suppress GA; other analytics remain optional and omit identity/billing/chat data.

Retention durations for account, billing/usage metadata, webhook IDs, backups and required records are undecided review gates. Authentication expiry cleanup exists; full account deletion/export/recovery is an operator process requiring review. Publish a concrete verified procedure before launch. Deleting an account must address recurring billing separately, and lawful record exceptions must be explained.

## Required review before publication/activation

- US federal/state recurring billing, cancellation, refund, consumer and privacy obligations; operating contact support@acquisitioncompanion.com.
- Tax registration/nexus/product classification and operator approval and U.S.-customer attestation for the initial invite-only paid beta. Do not enable automatic tax without that review.
- Portal period-end cancellation, payment-failure response, refunds/disputes and service outage policy.
- Retention, deletion/export, security incident/account recovery handling, processors and backups.
- No guarantee of financing, lender approval, acquisition success or professional advice; educational decision-support positioning.
- UK/EU/other paid expansion is out of scope until separate VAT/GST, consumer-law/tax/privacy review.
